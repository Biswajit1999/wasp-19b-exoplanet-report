# Changelog

## 2.1.0 — 2026-10-04

- Added a new scientifically informed artistic concept of WASP-19 b and its active late-G host.
- Labelled the image as conceptual, non-observational and not to scale in both public surfaces.
- Documented the measured system and literature cues behind the visualization while explicitly avoiding a resolved molecular or cloud-map claim.

## 2.0.0 — 2026-10-04

- Expanded the homogeneous 120-second TESS baseline from three sectors to the complete target-specific seven-sector inventory available at retrieval: Sectors 9, 36, 62, 63, 89, 90, and 99.
- Increased the timing sample from 89 to 204 supported transits and the baseline from 1,496 to 2,528 days.
- Added leave-one-sector-out ephemeris refits and a machine-readable sensitivity table.
- Added fail-closed SHA-256 checks for all committed FITS observations.
- Recalibrated the orbital-evolution conclusion: the linear ephemeris is preferred and no tidal-decay detection is claimed.
- Added primary-source context for the conflicting published atmospheric interpretations.
- Rebuilt the public site as a figure-led scientific report and removed the synthetic artist concept.
- Pinned CI actions to immutable commit SHAs and made the timing pipeline an explicit CI step.
